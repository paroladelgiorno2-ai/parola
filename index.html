Support

Parola del giorno teaches you one Italian word a day in three short lessons: a morning lesson to learn the word, an afternoon multiple-choice quiz, and a night Final Test.

Common questions

  • My lesson is locked. Each lesson unlocks at its reminder time. Change the times in Settings (the gear in the top right).
  • I'm not getting reminders. Turn on notifications for Parola in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications.
  • Can I change my level? Yes, in Settings. The new level starts with your next new word.
  • Light or dark colors? Choose Day, Night or Automatic in Settings → Appearance.
  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. The app has no accounts and doesn't use the internet.
