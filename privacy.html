Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Parola del giorno does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No internet. The app does not connect to the internet or send any data anywhere.
  • No tracking, analytics, or advertising.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. The words you've learned, your lesson times, your level and your appearance choice are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself; no outside service is used.
  • Motion sensor. It is used only to let the word pile at the bottom of the screen slide when you tilt your phone. This happens on the device and nothing is recorded.

Because no data leaves your iPhone, there is nothing for us to access, sell, or delete.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
